About Alberts Electric

Who We Are

Alberts Electric, operated by Alberts Electric LLC, provides electrical repair, upgrades, and new construction services for residential and commercial customers in Brookfield, Missouri and the surrounding areas.

We're locally owned and operated, which means when you call, you're talking directly to the people doing the work — not a call center. We show up, get the job done right, and treat your home or business like it's our own.
